How an enquiry works

Three steps, no surprises

1 · Pick a venue & date

Every venue page lists its season Saturdays. Booked dates are disabled and named — never a dead end: the nearest free Saturdays are suggested.

2 · Add a package & add-ons

Packages are priced per guest and combine with any venue. The total is itemised live: venue fee + per-guest × guests + add-ons.

3 · Send the enquiry

You receive a demo reference (for example WD-DEMO-####). Your date then shows as “Booked by you” on the venue page, and cancelling frees it again.
